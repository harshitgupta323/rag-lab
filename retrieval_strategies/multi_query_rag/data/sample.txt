Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to annual leave based on their employment
category and tenure.

Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Employees should submit their leave requests through the internal
employee portal.

Leave requests should normally be submitted at least five working days
before the planned leave date.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests.

In emergency situations, employees may submit a leave request with
shorter notice and should contact their manager as soon as possible.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward according to the company's
leave policy and applicable employment regulations.

Employees should contact the Human Resources department if they have
questions about their leave balance or eligibility.
